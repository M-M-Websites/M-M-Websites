Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated November 15, 2026

We want this to be simple and fair. Here is exactly when you can get money back, and how to stop your monthly plan.

On this page

  1. The $600 setup fee
  2. The $45 monthly maintenance fee
  3. How to cancel
  4. How refunds are paid
  5. After you cancel
  6. Questions or problems

The $600 setup fee

Before design work starts: if you change your mind before we begin designing your site, email us and we will refund the setup fee in full.

After design work starts: the setup fee is non-refundable, because the time and work have already gone into your site.

The $45 monthly maintenance fee

Your plan is month to month, with no contract. You can cancel anytime. Cancelling stops all future billing. Monthly fees are not prorated, so we do not refund part of a month that has already been charged, and your maintenance continues until the end of that paid month.

How to cancel

Email mnmwebsitesforyou@gmail.com from the address you signed up with and say you would like to cancel. You can also manage your billing yourself through Manage billing: sign in with the email you paid with and Stripe sends you a one-time link. We will confirm the cancellation by email.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds go back to the card, Apple Pay or Link account you paid with, through Stripe. They usually appear within 5 to 10 business days, depending on your bank.

After you cancel

When your paid month ends, your site is taken offline and is no longer public, the domain is paused and updates stop. The website, its code and the domain belong to M&M Websites, so they aren’t handed over or transferred. Your own logo, photos and words stay yours.

Questions or problems

If something is not right, email mnmwebsitesforyou@gmail.com. We would rather fix it than lose your trust.
