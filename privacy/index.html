Privacy Policy

Last updated November 15, 2026

This policy explains what information M&M Websites collects, why, and what we do with it. The short version: we collect only what we need to talk with you and do the work, and we never sell it.

Who we are

M&M Websites is a sole proprietorship based in Georgia, USA. For any privacy question or request, email mnmwebsitesforyou@gmail.com.

What we collect

When you use the "Start a project" form: your name, email address, business name, website (if you have one) and whatever you tell us about your project.

When you pay: your name, email, billing details and payment information are collected by Stripe. We see your name, email and payment status, but never your full card number.

When you visit the site: basic, anonymous visit statistics such as pages viewed, browser type and country.

When you email us: your email address and the contents of your messages.

How the form works

Our project form is delivered by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a service that forwards what you type to our email inbox. FormSubmit processes your submission to deliver it. We use what you send only to reply to you and plan your project.

Analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand how many people visit and which pages they read. It is privacy-focused: it does not use cookies, does not track you across other websites and does not build a profile of you. Our site is also delivered through Cloudflare, which processes basic technical data such as IP addresses to keep it fast and secure.

How we use your information

To answer your questions, build and maintain your website, send invoices and receipts, keep our records and meet legal or tax requirements. We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

Only the services that help us run the business: Stripe (payments), FormSubmit (form delivery), Cloudflare (hosting and analytics) and Google (our email). Each handles data under its own privacy policy. We may also share information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep project emails and form messages for as long as we are working together and for a reasonable time afterwards. Payment records are kept as long as tax and accounting rules require.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by emailing mnmwebsitesforyou@gmail.com. We will reply within 30 days. Some records, such as payment history, may need to be kept for legal reasons.

Cookies

This site does not set its own tracking or advertising cookies. Stripe may use cookies on its checkout pages to process payments safely and prevent fraud.

Children

Our services are for businesses. This site is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. Important changes will be emailed to active clients.
