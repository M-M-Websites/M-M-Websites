Who we are

M&M Websites is a sole proprietorship based in Georgia, USA. We design and build websites for local service businesses. You can reach us any time at mnmwebsitesforyou@gmail.com.

What you get

A custom website designed and built for your business, plus ongoing maintenance: updates, fixes and small changes while your plan is active. The exact scope of your site (pages, features, launch date) is agreed with you by email before work starts.

Price and billing

The service costs $600 one time for setup, plus $45 a month for maintenance. At signup you pay the setup fee and your first month together. After that, $45 is billed automatically each month to the payment method you signed up with.

Payments are processed securely by Stripe, by credit or debit card, Apple Pay or Link. Checkout asks you to accept these terms before you pay. Stripe emails you a receipt for every payment. We never see or store your full card number.

If a payment fails, Stripe automatically retries it, and you can update your payment details in Manage billing. We may pause maintenance until billing is up to date, and if the plan ends unpaid, section 5 applies.

Month to month, cancel anytime

There is no contract or minimum term. You can cancel your monthly plan at any time by emailing us. You can also manage your billing yourself through Manage billing, Stripe’s secure billing page. Cancelling stops future charges, and your site goes offline when your paid month ends (see section 5). See our Refund and Cancellation Policy for how refunds work.
