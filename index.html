Questions and answers

Price, payment, ownership and launch. Still unsure? Ask us directly.

How much does a website cost?

$600 one time to design and build your site, plus $45 a month for maintenance. You pay $645 at signup (the setup fee and your first month), then $45 each month after that. See what’s included.

How can I pay?

By credit or debit card, Apple Pay or Link, on Stripe’s secure checkout. You’ll accept our Terms of Service at checkout, and Stripe emails you a receipt for every payment.

How long does it take?

It depends on the number of pages, the features and how quickly content like photos and service lists comes together. You’ll get a timeline after our first conversation, and the process above shows each step.

Do I own the website?

No. The website, its code, the hosting and the domain belong to M&M Websites, and they can’t be transferred or handed to someone else. Your monthly plan is what keeps the site online and up to date. Your own logo, photos and words stay yours.

What happens if I stop paying?

If the monthly plan is cancelled or payments stop, the site is taken offline and is no longer public, the domain is paused, and updates stop. There’s no access to the code after that. If a monthly payment fails, Stripe automatically tries again, so you have time to update your card in Manage billing.

How do I update my card, see receipts or cancel?

Use Manage billing and sign in with the email you paid with. Stripe sends you a one-time sign-in link, then you can update your payment details and see past payments. You can also email us at mnmwebsitesforyou@gmail.com to cancel or for help.

Can you help with my domain, hosting and Google Business Profile?

Yes. Launch help covers choosing and connecting a domain, setting up hosting, and setting up or tidying your Google Business Profile so it points to the new site.

I already have a website. Can you redesign it?

Yes. Include the address in the form and we’ll look at what’s working, what isn’t and what’s worth keeping before we start.

What do you need from me?

Your logo if you have one, photos of your work, the services you offer and how you want customers to reach you. If some of that doesn’t exist yet, we’ll help you work out what’s needed.

Will you update the site after launch?

Yes. Ongoing care covers changes like new photos, services, hours or prices, so the site keeps up with your business.
