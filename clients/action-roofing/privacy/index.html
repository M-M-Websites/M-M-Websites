Privacy policy

This page explains what information the Action Roofing website collects and how it’s used. Last updated November 2026.

What we collect

The only personal information this website collects is what you choose to type into the estimate form: your name, phone number, email address, the property’s ZIP code or address, the service you need, your description and any photos you attach, and how you’d like to be contacted.

How it’s used

Action Roofing uses it only to reply to your request and talk with you about your project. We don’t sell it, we don’t add you to a mailing list, and we don’t use it for advertising.

How the form sends it

When the form is connected, your request is delivered to Action Roofing by email through a form-delivery service over an encrypted connection. Until then, the form doesn’t send anything. You can always call (931) 645-4784 instead.

Cookies and tracking

This website doesn’t set advertising or tracking cookies. Its host, Cloudflare, counts page visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and doesn’t identify you. The host also keeps standard server logs, such as IP addresses and the pages requested, to keep the site running and secure.

Links to other sites

Some pages link to other websites, such as the BBB and Clarksville Now. Their own privacy policies apply when you visit them.

Your choices

To ask what we have from a form you sent, or to have it deleted, call (931) 645-4784 or email actionroofing@gmail.com.
