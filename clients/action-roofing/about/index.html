About Action Roofing

Action Roofing is a Clarksville roofing business led by Robert DeBord. We help property owners address roofing problems and plan exterior improvements, with roofing, siding, and gutter services tailored to the work their property needs. Serving Clarksville since 1985.

After a contractor stopped returning his calls, Army veteran Keith Cherry got a new roof at no cost to him. Clarksville Now reported that Action Roofing, Windstar Roofing and ABC Supply teamed up to do the job: the two roofing companies contributed the labor, and ABC Supply donated the materials.
