What goes into a shingle roof

A roof is more than the shingles you see from the street. Scroll to watch one go on, layer by layer.

  1. 01Roof deckThe plywood or plank base that everything else is fastened to.
  2. 02Drip edgeMetal edging along the eaves and rakes that carries water off the edge and into the gutter.
  3. 03UnderlaymentA water-resistant layer rolled out over the deck: a second line of defense under the shingles.
  4. 04Starter stripA first row along the eave that seals the bottom edge so wind can’t get under it.
  5. 05ShinglesLaid in overlapping courses from the eave up, each row covering the nails of the row below.
  6. 06Ridge capCaps folded over the peak, where the two slopes meet.
  7. 07GuttersCatch the water at the eave and carry it away from the walls and foundation.

Every roof is different. Ask what yours needs when you request an estimate.

Questions about your roof

Straight answers to the questions people ask first. Anything else, just call.

Do you repair roofs, or only replace them?

Both. Action Roofing handles roof repairs and full roof replacements. If you’re not sure which one your roof needs, call (931) 645-4784 and describe what you’re seeing.

Can you help after a storm?

Yes. Storm-related roof repairs are part of the work Action Roofing does. If it’s safe, take photos from the ground, then call or send them with the estimate form. Please don’t climb onto a damaged roof.

Do you work on siding and gutters too?

Yes. Along with roofing, Action Roofing works on siding and gutters. Tell us what you’re noticing and we’ll talk through it.

Where do you work?
