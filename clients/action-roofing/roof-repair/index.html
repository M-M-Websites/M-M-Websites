Signs your roof needs a repair

If any of these sound familiar, it’s worth a call.

  • Water stains on a ceiling or in the attic
  • Shingles that are missing, cracked or curling
  • Shingle granules collecting in the gutters
  • A drip or a damp spot after heavy rain
  • Daylight through the roof boards in the attic
  • Damage after wind, hail or a falling limb

Repair or replace?

Sometimes a repair is all a roof needs. Other times the damage is spread across the roof, or the roof is near the end of its life, and patching it means paying twice. Ask what we’d recommend and why, and decide from there.

Roof repair: common questions

Straight answers to the questions people ask first. Anything else, just call.

Can you fix just one part of the roof?

Often a repair can focus on the damaged area. Whether that’s the right call depends on the condition of the rest of the roof, which we’ll talk through with you.

Should I go up on the roof to look?

Please don’t. Roofs are steep and slick, and damaged areas can give way. Take photos from the ground and leave the climbing to us.

How soon can you come out?

Contact us to discuss availability. Call (931) 645-4784 and we’ll talk about timing.
