    Questions

    What do you haul?

    Household junk, furniture, appliances, move-out leftovers, yard debris and landscaping debris, plus landscape supply transport and other hauling jobs by request. Not sure about something? Send a photo and ask.

    Do you do the loading?

    Yes. Two Leos loads the items and takes them away. You don’t have to drag anything to the curb.

    How much does it cost?

    Every job is different: how much there is, what it’s made of and how far it has to be carried all matter. Send a few photos with the quote form, or call or text, and Two Leos will give you a price for your job.

    How big is the trailer?

    Two Leos hauls with a 14-cubic-yard dump trailer. That measures space, not weight, so heavy materials can fill it up faster. Ask if you’re not sure your load will fit.

    Is there anything you can’t take?

    Some items and materials may not be accepted, depending on what they are and how much they weigh. Tell us what you have and you’ll know before the job.

    What areas do you serve?

    Two Leos Hauling is based in Spokane, Washington. If you’re outside Spokane, ask: include your ZIP code in the quote form.

    When are you available?

    Two Leos is listed as open daily from 8 AM to 9 PM. Pick a preferred date in the quote form and you’ll hear back to confirm a time.

    How do I send photos?

    Add them to the quote form on this site, or text them to (509) 688-5071.
