About Two Leos Hauling

A Spokane junk removal and hauling business. You show us what needs to go; we load it and haul it away.

Illustrative photo

What Two Leos does

Two Leos Hauling LLC takes on the jobs nobody wants to do themselves: junk removal, yard debris, move-out cleanups, furniture and appliance disposal, landscape supply transport and general hauling around Spokane, Washington.

The difference is the work is included. This isn’t a trailer rental. The crew loads the items and takes them away.

The equipment

Jobs are hauled in a 14-cubic-yard dump trailer, big enough for furniture, appliances, cleanouts and yard debris. Remember that 14 yards is space, not weight: what fits depends on the material.
